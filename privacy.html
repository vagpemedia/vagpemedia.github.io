Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

VAGPE Media ("we", "us") operates an internal video production pipeline that publishes short-form video content to YouTube and Instagram on behalf of the VAGPE Media channel.

What we access

Our software connects to the YouTube Data API using Google OAuth solely to upload video content to the VAGPE Media YouTube channel. We request only the permission needed to upload videos on this channel's behalf. We do not access, read, or store any other Google account data.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal data from viewers of our content.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any account data to third parties.
  • We do not use any data obtained through Google APIs for advertising purposes.

Data retention

OAuth credentials are stored securely and used only by our internal render pipeline to publish videos. They are not shared outside the organization.
